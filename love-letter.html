My Love.

A quieter room inside the same story.

My Love,

I’m writing this letter not to blame you, pressure you, or make you feel guilty. I’m writing because there are things I simply can’t leave unsaid.

I miss you.

I miss having you beside me, our conversations, our little moments, and the feeling of sharing my life with you.

I know that a lot of things built up between us. I know that we hurt each other, and I know that I made mistakes. I have had a lot of time to think about those mistakes, and I am sorry for the ways in which I hurt you.

I don't expect you to forget what happened, and I don't expect you to make any decision now.

I only want you to know that my love for you was real, and it still is.

If one day, you would like to talk, I would be willing to sit down with you calmly — without blame, without pride, and without keeping score of the past.

I don't know what the future holds for us.
But I hope that whatever happens, we can remember that what we had was real and that we mattered to each other.

I love you.

Niki

P.S. The headphones are your birthday gift from me. You didn't need to leave them behind. They are yours, and so is my love for you.
