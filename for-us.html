For Us

Baby.

Words from my heart, for us.

Baby,

I miss you.

I miss having you beside me. I miss our conversations, our little moments, our mornings, our evenings, and that feeling of having the person I chose to share my life with right there next to me.

But over the past few days, I’ve started thinking about something else too.

I’ve started looking back not only through my own eyes, but trying to see our story through yours.

And when I do, I don’t just see the beautiful things we had. I also see the moments when I hurt you. I see things you were probably carrying inside while I didn’t fully understand what I was putting you through.

And that hurts me.

It hurts not only because I lost you, but because the person I love was hurt by me.

I know that a lot of things built up between us. I know that we both made mistakes, and I know that I have my share of responsibility for what happened.

I don’t want to make excuses.

And I don’t want to use this letter to change the past. I can’t.

All I can do is be honest with you about what I understand now.

I understand that you can truly love someone and still make them feel alone.

And I understand that if I ever get the chance to be by your side again, I don’t want to simply love you the way I did before.

I want to love you better.

I want to listen more. To understand more. To talk when something is hurting me instead of letting it build up inside. To cherish the person beside me instead of taking his presence for granted.

And that’s why I want to tell you something very clearly.

I want you. I want us. I want us to try again.

I don’t want us to simply go back to what we were.

I want us to build something better than what we had before.

I can’t promise you that everything will be easy. I can’t promise you that I’ll never make another mistake.

But I can promise you something more important — if you give me that chance, I will never take your love for granted.

And I don’t want to prove that to you with words alone.

I want to show you through the way I treat you.

I don’t expect you to forget what happened. I don’t expect you to erase your pain just because I understand more now.

But I want you to know where I stand.

I still choose you.

Sometimes I think about the most ordinary moments we shared, and somehow those are the ones I miss the most.

A simple breakfast. Tomatoes and cucumbers. Eggs. Talking about nothing and everything.

The moments when you were simply there.

And the way you used to look at me.

I remember waking you up in the mornings with a thousand kisses, while you were still half asleep, barely opening your eyes as my lips wandered all over your face.

I remember waiting for you at the door with a drink, wearing nothing but my briefs, ready to welcome you home with a kiss.

I remember how I used to keep bothering you to eat your bread — and somehow, even that is something I miss now.

And I remember how almost every time I lay down on the bed, you would come over, throw yourself on top of me, wrap your arms around me, and simply fall asleep there.

They may sound like such small things, but they were our everyday life. Our closeness. That quiet feeling that somehow, in each other’s arms, we were home.

And when I think about you now, it isn’t only the big moments that I miss.

I miss exactly this.

I miss our “us.”

Maybe those little things are the greatest proof of just how real all of this was for me.

I don’t know what the future will bring us.

But I know what I would like to have in mine.

You.

I want us to look back at this moment one day and not remember it only as the end of something beautiful.

I want us to be able to look back on it as the moment when we stopped, realized what we had lost, and found the courage to make it better.

I don’t want to take you back into the past.

I want us to move forward — together.

I love you.

And I believe that what we have between us deserves at least one more chance to be heard.

Niki
